# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

ElementSmith is committed to a respectful, constructive and inclusive project environment.

Positive participation includes:

- giving specific, actionable and empathetic feedback;
- respecting different experience levels, backgrounds and perspectives;
- discussing technical decisions without attacking people;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them openly;
- prioritising the safety and accessibility of the wider community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person’s private information.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and any public space where someone represents ElementSmith.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact options on the owner’s GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential where possible.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behaviour conflicts with this standard.
